Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

About this application

rclone-drive is a private, personal-use configuration for accessing the owner's Google Drive account using rclone. It is not offered to the public.

Information accessed

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the owner's Google Drive account. Depending on the permissions granted, rclone may access files and folders in that account.

How information is used

Information is used solely to provide file access and synchronization between the owner's computer and Google Drive.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and configuration are stored locally on the owner's computer. This project does not operate a separate server that collects or stores user data. Information is not sold or shared with third parties by this project.

Third-party services

Google Drive is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Data deletion

The owner can revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings and delete the locally stored rclone configuration and credentials.

Contact

This is a personal project maintained by its owner. For questions, contact the owner through their GitHub account.
